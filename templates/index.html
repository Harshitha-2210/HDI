The Four Tiers

How the reading is classified

Once the model estimates an HDI score, it's placed into one of four development tiers for a simple policy-facing interpretation.

0.000 – 0.549

Low

Significant gaps in basic services and infrastructure call for coordinated intervention.

0.550 – 0.699

Medium

Emerging economies with room to improve across multiple service dimensions.

0.700 – 0.799

High

Solid, broad-based development with strong access and health outcomes.

0.800 – 1.000

Very High

Strong access, health, and labor conditions working together.

Under the Hood

How a prediction is made

  1. Enter the ten indicators

    Electricity access, internet users, water access, health expenditure, female labor force participation, unemployment, infant mortality, fertility rate, urban population, and CO2 emissions.

  2. The model scores the inputs

    The training pipeline compares a standardized linear model with a random forest on countries it has never seen, and saves the stronger performer.

  3. The needle settles on a reading

    The predicted score is classified into a tier and shown on the instrument, along with what it implies for policy priorities.
